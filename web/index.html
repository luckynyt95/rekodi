Ulinzi · Guardrails

  • 🚫 Haichunguzi. Kamwe haipendekezi ugonjwa au dawa. Never diagnoses or suggests treatment.
  • 👩‍⚕️ Binadamu anaamua. Hakuna kinachohifadhiwa bila uthibitisho wako. Human decides. Nothing saves without your confirm.
  • ❓ "Sina uhakika — uliza mtu". Sehemu zisizo na uhakika zina alama. "Not sure — ask a person" badges on low-confidence fields.
  • 📴 Inafanya kazi bila intaneti. Data inakaa kwenye simu. Works offline. Data stays on this phone.
  • 📵 Simu inapotea? Rekodi zimehifadhiwa kama maandishi ya kawaida — futa programu au weka upya simu. Lost phone? Records are plain JSON — wipe the app or reset the phone.

Data inakaa wapi · Where data sits

Kwenye simu hii tu (outbox.json), hadi utakapotuma kwa mfumo wa wizara (DHIS2). Inasomwa na muuguzi na wasimamizi wa kliniki tu. On this phone only, until you sync to the ministry system (DHIS2). Read only by the nurse and clinic supervisors.
