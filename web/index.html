Karibu · Welcome

Rekodi inasaidia muuguzi kurekodi ziara kwa sauti ya Kiswahili, kisha kuandaa muhtasari — hakuna uchunguzi, hakuna dawa inapendekezwa. Rekodi helps the nurse record visits by Swahili voice, then drafts the record — no diagnosis, no treatment suggested.

Ziara mpya · New visit

Rekodi sauti ya Kiswahili (~dakika 1). Hufanya kazi bila intaneti. Record a Swahili voice note (~1 min). Works offline.

Hakiki · Review

⚠️ Rekodi haichunguzi wala kupendekeza dawa. Inarekodi tu ulichosema. Angalia kila sehemu na uthibitishe. Rekodi does not diagnose or suggest treatment. It only records what you said. Check every field, then confirm.

Sanduku la kutoka · Outbox

Rekodi zilizohifadhiwa kwenye simu. Tuma DHIS2 unapopata intaneti. Records stored on this phone. Sync to DHIS2 when you have connectivity.
